 Students’ Monitored Canteen (SMC)

 Operational Framework

The **Students’ Monitored Canteen (SMC) at IISER Kolkata** is founded on a simple principle:

 “Made by the students, of the students, and for the students.” 

The SMC is a student-run initiative under the *SAC Food, Health and Hygiene (FHH) division*, with the objective of providing the student community with **affordable, hygienic, good-quality, and accessible food**.

The SMC consists of approximately *70 workers*, including the  *Mess Manager and Mess Accountant*, who are responsible for the day-to-day functioning of the mess.

 

À La Carte Dining System

The SMC operates on an *à la carte system*, where students can choose individual food items from the available menu rather than being required to purchase a fixed meal.

Each item is listed separately with its corresponding price. Students can therefore *choose what they want, decide the quantity they require, and pay accordingly*.

This system provides greater flexibility in terms of food preferences, quantity, and spending. It also allows the SMC to introduce a wider variety of dishes and modify the menu according to *student demand, seasonal availability, ingredient prices, and feedback*.

At the same time, an à la carte system requires careful coordination between **menu planning, pricing, procurement, inventory management, and quality control** to ensure that food remains affordable while the mess remains financially sustainable.

---
SMC Sub-Committees & Functional Areas

The functioning of the SMC is divided among different student sub-committees and functional teams. Each team is responsible for a specific aspect of the mess.

 1. Menu & Price Review

Responsible for reviewing and updating the menu based on student preferences, feedback, seasonal availability, nutritional considerations, ingredient prices, and demand.

The objective is to provide sufficient variety while keeping food affordable.

 2. Quality Check

Responsible for monitoring the quality and hygiene of food served in the mess.

This includes:

* Checking the quality of raw materials
* Monitoring food preparation and cooking
* Checking kitchen hygiene and sanitation
* Monitoring food storage and handling
* Checking the quality of food being served

 3. Market Survey

Responsible for surveying local markets and suppliers to monitor:

* Prices of raw materials
* Availability of ingredients
* Quality of products
* Seasonal produce
* Potential vendors and suppliers

This helps the SMC make informed procurement decisions and source good-quality ingredients at reasonable prices.

 4. Workers’ Grievance Cell

Responsible for providing a channel through which mess workers can raise their concerns.

The cell works to:

* Listen to workers’ concerns
* Communicate grievances to the appropriate authority
* Assist in resolving workplace-related issues
* Maintain communication between workers and the student committee
* Promote a safe and respectful working environment

 5. Communication & Students’ Grievance Cell

Acts as the primary communication channel between the SMC and the student community.

Its responsibilities include:

* Collecting student feedback
* Receiving and documenting grievances
* Communicating mess-related updates
* Following up on complaints
* Communicating student suggestions to relevant committees
* Ensuring that genuine concerns are addressed

The objective is to create a **continuous feedback system** through which student concerns can result in practical improvements.

 6. Repairs & Maintenance

Responsible for monitoring the physical infrastructure and facilities of the mess, including:

* Kitchen equipment
* Electrical systems
* Lighting
* Seating
* Plumbing
* General infrastructure

The team coordinates with the appropriate personnel or authorities to ensure that reported issues are followed up and resolved.

 7. Administration & Finance

Responsible for the administrative and financial functioning of the SMC, including:

* Budgeting and financial planning
* Monitoring expenses and cash flow
* Vendor payments
* Maintaining financial records
* Procurement-related coordination
* Other administrative requirements

Coordination with Ex-SAC FHH

The SMC coordinates with the *Ex-SAC FHH team* to maintain continuity between different tenures.

This includes following up on *tasks, initiatives, and responsibilities that were initiated during the previous tenure but remain incomplete*.

This ensures that ongoing work is not discontinued simply because there has been a change in the student committee, and that previously initiated responsibilities are brought to completion.

 8. Cyber Cell

Responsible for the digital requirements of the SMC, including:

* Online feedback systems
* Digital communication
* Payment-related infrastructure
* Online forms
* Digital records
* Official online information

 9. Institute Orders

Responsible for coordinating with the **IISER Kolkata administration** regarding institute-level requirements, permissions, official orders, and other matters requiring institutional involvement.

The team ensures that relevant institute directives are communicated and implemented appropriately.

 10. Misconduct Handling

Responsible for addressing behavioural concerns, misconduct, and operational disputes related to the functioning of the mess.

Such matters should be handled **fairly, transparently, and in accordance with the applicable rules and procedures**, while maintaining a safe and respectful environment for students and workers.

---

 Core Principles of the SMC

 Student Autonomy

The SMC is student-driven and managed around the needs and requirements of the student community.

 Affordable Accessibility

The mess aims to provide food at reasonable prices and give students flexibility through the à la carte system.

 Food Quality & Hygiene

Food quality, hygiene, sanitation, and safe food handling remain fundamental priorities.

 Transparency & Accountability

Responsibilities are distributed among different committees and functional teams, allowing issues to be tracked and addressed appropriately.

 Student Participation

Students are not merely consumers of the mess. They are an integral part of its functioning through *feedback, participation, grievance reporting, and involvement in SMC committees*.

 Continuity of Work

The SMC maintains continuity between different student tenures by following up on ongoing initiatives and completing responsibilities initiated by previous committees.

 Continuous Improvement

The SMC follows a continuous feedback approach:

*Student Feedback → Discussion → Verification → Action → Resolution → Follow-up*

The objective is to ensure that the mess continuously evolves according to the actual needs and experiences of its students.

 

 Grievance Management

A grievance is not simply a complaint; it can help identify a gap in the existing system.

The SMC therefore aims to follow a systematic process:

*Complaint/Feedback → Documentation → Verification → Relevant Committee/Authority → Action → Resolution → Follow-up*

This ensures that concerns raised by students or workers are properly recorded, considered, and addressed rather than being treated as isolated incidents.

Ultimately, the SMC aims to create a mess that is **student-driven, affordable, hygienic, transparent, accountable, and responsive** to the needs of the campus community.
