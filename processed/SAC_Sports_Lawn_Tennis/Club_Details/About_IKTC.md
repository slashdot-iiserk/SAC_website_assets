# About Iktc

SHORT INTRODUCTION


IKTC, short for IISER Kolkata Tennis Club, encourages students, as well as staff and professors to come together for a fresh breath of air from the relentless academic world. The club motivates and fosters growth of tennis among the community. Tennis is not just about physical well-being, it helps develop a sense of a calculative and strategic mind as well as teamwork, leadership qualities among people. People can engage in friendly play and also in a competitive environment. Everyone, from advanced players to absolute beginners are highly encouraged to engage in the activities of the club and contribute to its growth.


OBJECTIVES


To bring together players of all skill levels and give everyone, beginners and pros alike, a space to learn, improve, and have fun.

To offer a much-needed break from academic life and highlight the physical, mental, and social benefits tennis has to offer.

To keep making IISER Kolkata proud at IISM and competitions at other institutes, building on our strong medal-winning streak.

To connect beginners with seniors and create a supportive environment where everyone helps each other grow.

To steadily improve our facilities, equipment, and court access so the experience keeps getting better for all members.

To build a community where showing up matters just as much as winning, and everyone feels welcome.


ACHIEVEMENTS


IKTC has always made a mark at IISM events. We have continuously brought in gold and silver medals in almost all the categories (9 medals) in 2022-2024, even without any formal coaching.

We have been able to instill an interest among the beginners as well.
