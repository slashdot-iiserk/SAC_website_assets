# Events And Activities

IKCC (CHESS CLUB OF IISER KOLKATA) IISER KOLKATA EVENTS AND ACTIVITIES

Events & Tournaments

1. Fresher Match

A welcome match organized for newly admitted students to introduce them to the chess culture at IISER Kolkata and encourage participation in club activities.

2. IISM Practice Sessions

Structured training sessions conducted throughout the year to improve chess fundamentals, tactics, critical thinking, and tournament readiness.

3. Practice Matches with Other Colleges

Friendly matches organized with neighboring institutions to provide competitive exposure and help players gain real-game pressure and experience.

4. IISM

Participation in the Inter IISER Sports Meet, where the IISER Kolkata chess team represents the institute in national-level inter-IISER competition.

5. Interbatch Matches

An intra-campus tournament involving different academic batches, promoting healthy competition, interaction, and team spirit among students.

6. Inquesta 1v1

A chess event organized during Inquviesta that encourages wider student participation from both inside and outside the campus in a fast-paced and exciting format.


7. All-Star Matches

Exhibition games featuring standout performers from various teams, celebrating talent and fostering community engagement within the club.


8. Auction Tournament

A franchise-style tournament where captains select players through an auction process, creating balanced teams and a unique competitive experience.

9. Farewell Matches

Special matches organized to celebrate graduating students and recognize their contributions to the basketball club.

Club Activities

● Organizes regular chess tournaments throughout the academic year, catering to players of all skill levels.

● Conducts events with various time controls, including Classical, Rapid, Blitz, and Bullet formats, to provide diverse competitive experiences.

● Hosts Inter-Batch Chess Championships to encourage participation and foster a competitive spirit among students.

● Organizes the Open Chess Tournament as part of Inquivesta, providing a platform for wider engagement with the chess community.

● Holds practice sessions, friendly matches, and puzzle-solving activities to help members improve their strategic and analytical skills.

● Prepares and trains students for inter-institute competitions such as IISM, ensuring strong representation of IISER Kolkata at national-level events.
