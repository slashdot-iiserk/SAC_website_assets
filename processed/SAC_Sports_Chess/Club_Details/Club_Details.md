​IKCC ( CHESS CLUB OF IISER KOLKATA)​
​Short introduction​
​The Chess Club of IISER Kolkata is a vibrant community of students brought​
​together by their passion for the game of chess. The club provides a platform for​
​players of all skill levels to learn, practice, and compete in a friendly and​
​intellectually stimulating environment. Through regular casual games, training​
​sessions, puzzles, and tournaments, the club fosters strategic thinking,​
​sportsmanship, and camaraderie among its members. Whether you are a​
​seasoned player or a beginner eager to learn, the Chess Club welcomes everyone​
​to explore the endless possibilities of the 64 squares.​
​Objectives​
​1.​ ​To promote interest and participation in chess among students of IISER​
​Kolkata.​
​2.​ ​To provide opportunities for players of all skill levels to learn, practice, and​
​improve their game.​
​3.​ ​To foster critical thinking, strategic planning, problem-solving, and​
​decision-making skills through chess.​
​4.​ ​To organize regular tournaments, friendly matches, workshops, and​
​training sessions.​
​5.​ ​To encourage healthy competition, sportsmanship, and mutual respect​
​among members.​
​6.​ ​To represent IISER Kolkata in inter-college, state-level, and national chess​
​competitions.​
​7.​ ​To create an inclusive and supportive community where students can​
​connect through a shared passion for chess.​

​Major Activities​
​1.​ ​Regular practice sessions and coaching programs for club members.​
​2.​ ​Selection and training of institute teams for inter-college and inter-IISER​
​tournaments.​
​3.​ ​Organization of intra-campus chess competitions and friendly matches.​
​4.​ ​Conducting introductory sessions and recruitment drives for new students.​
​5.​ ​Participation in sports festivals, invitational tournaments, and​
​institute-level sporting events.​
​6.​ ​Promoting chess culture through workshops, exhibitions, and community​
​engagement activities.​
​Major Achievements​
​1.​ ​The club has been an active participant in the Inter IISER Sports Meet​
​(IISM), consistently representing IISER Kolkata at the national level.​
​2.​ ​The IISER Kolkata Chess Team has achieved podium finishes in IISM for​
​the past three consecutive years, demonstrating sustained excellence and​
​competitive strength.​
​3.​ ​The club regularly organizes Inter-Batch Chess Tournaments, fostering​
​healthy competition and encouraging greater participation among​
​students.​
​4.​ ​The Chess Club successfully conducts the Open Chess Tournament during​
​Inquivesta, attracting participants from both within and outside the​
​institute community.​
​5.​ ​Throughout the academic year, the club organizes numerous chess​
​tournaments featuring diverse time controls, including Classical, Rapid,​
​Blitz, and other innovative formats.​
​With a strong record of competitive success and a commitment to promoting chess within the​
​institute, the Chess Club of IISER Kolkata continues to be a vibrant and integral part of campus life.​
