# About the SAC Placement Cell

The SAC Placement Cell is the student-run body under SAC Academics that coordinates
the campus placement season at IISER Kolkata. Acting as the bridge between the
institute's career office, the visiting companies, and the graduating cohort, the cell
takes responsibility for every stage of the recruitment drive — from announcing
recruiters and circulating resumes to running the Pre-Placement Talks, handling
logistics on the day of each visit, and publishing the final selection results.

The committee is composed of an Overall Coordinator, Department Coordinators drawn
from each academic stream (Physical Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Biological Sciences,
Mathematics and Statistics, and Earth Sciences), and a small group of Department
Volunteers who help with scheduling, communications, and on-the-day coordination. The
current term runs for the 2025-26 academic year.
