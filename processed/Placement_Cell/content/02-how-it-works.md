# How the Placement Process Works

The campus placement process at IISER Kolkata is run jointly by the Career
Development Cell and the SAC Placement Student Committee. The committee handles
the student-facing parts of the season — communications, scheduling, and on-the-day
logistics — so that every eligible candidate has a clear, consistent experience
from the announcement of a visiting company to the final offer.

## The typical recruitment cycle

1. Announcement. The committee circulates the recruiter's job description and
   eligibility criteria across the relevant batches. A short registration window
   follows, after which the resumes are forwarded to the company.
2. Pre-Placement Talk. Most companies host a PPT in the lecture-hall complex
   before the assessment. Attendance is optional but strongly recommended — it is
   the easiest way to read the team, the role, and the expectations for the day.
3. Online Assessment. Written or online tests are usually scheduled in the
   central computer lab or the institute auditorium, with timings shared a day
   in advance.
4. Interviews. Shortlisted candidates are called for technical and HR rounds.
   The committee coordinates interview slots, room allocations, and panel
   logistics on the day of the visit.
5. Results. Final selections are announced through the committee's official
   channels. The Career Development Cell then processes the offers and
   onboarding paperwork.

## Who can participate

Participation is open to all registered BS-MS and PhD students of IISER Kolkata
who are in their final year and meet the eligibility criteria shared by the
recruiter. Students in earlier years may register for internships where the
company permits, subject to academic-load considerations.

## What the committee handles

- Communicating job descriptions, deadlines, and shortlists to students.
- Coordinating Pre-Placement Talks, assessments, and interview schedules.
- Reserving rooms, equipment, and technical support for each visit.
- Acting as a first point of contact for student concerns during the
  recruitment season.
- Maintaining a feedback channel with the Career Development Cell so that
  process issues raised in one season are addressed in the next.
