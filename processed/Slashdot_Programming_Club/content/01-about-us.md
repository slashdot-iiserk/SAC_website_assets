# About Us

![Slashdot — The Coding & Designing Club of IISER Kolkata](../images/branding/slashdot-logo-banner.webp)

- **Founded:** Conceptualized in 2019, officially formed on January 8th, 2021.
- **Initiated By:** Dedicated members from the 16MS, 17MS, 18MS, and 19MS batches.
- **Mission:** To create a community where people can learn coding and graphic design while having fun.
- **Relevance:** Coding, Design and AI skills are essential today and beneficial for future endeavors.
- **Open Invitation:** Welcomes all who are interested in coding and designing.
