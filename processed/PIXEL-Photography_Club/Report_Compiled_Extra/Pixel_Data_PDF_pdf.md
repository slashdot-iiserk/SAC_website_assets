1. Introduction To Club
Pixel – The Photography Club of IISER Kolkata, consisting of both phone and DSLR photographers, is
a space where students, faculty, and staff come together over a shared love for photography—not just to
take pictures, but to learn how to truly see. For us, photography is as much about observation as it is
about the camera, about finding meaning in everyday moments and turning them into something worth
remembering. Most of our members are amateur photographers, and the club grows through shared
experiences where people experiment, learn from each other, and gradually develop their own way of
looking at the world, making photography both an art and an enjoyable hobby.

4. Events
FLAGSHIP EVENTS
1. Photowalks
“‘ Photowalks are group photography excursions that help participants develop observation, com-
position, and storytelling skills through practical experience. Pixel organizes three to four pho-
towalks annually, often visiting nearby cities and culturally significant locations. These outings not
only provide valuable learning opportunities but have also contributed significantly to the club’s
achievements, with several IICM-winning photographs originating from photowalk submissions.
2. PHOTON
PHOTON is the flagship photography event conducted during Inquivesta, the annual fest of IISER
Kolkata. The event invites participation from colleges across India, providing a platform for pho-
tographers to showcase their creativity and compete at a national level. Outstanding entries are
recognized and rewarded through prizes and certificates.
3. Shuttercycle
Shuttercycle is a weekly or monthly themed photography challenge designed to encourage creative
exploration and consistent engagement with photography. Participants submit photographs based
on a given theme, emphasizing originality, composition, and visual storytelling.
The initiative
fosters artistic growth within the community while providing recognition through feature posts
and shout-outs on the club’s social media platforms. “‘

6. Rules
Equipment Issuing Rules
1. Ownership All equipment issued by Pixel Photography Club remains the property of the club.
Borrowing equipment does not confer any ownership rights to the borrower.
2. Eligibility
Equipment may be borrowed only by currently enrolled IISER Kolkata students who have either
attended the camera handling workshop or possess prior experience.
A completed Equipment
Lending Form, signed by a Pixel Office Bearer and the SAC Cultural General Secretary (Cult GS),
must be submitted to the Dean of Students (DoS) Office before issuance.
3. Inspection
Borrowers must inspect the equipment for any defects or damage before accepting it and report
any issues immediately. Failure to do so will be deemed acceptance of the equipment in satisfactory
condition.
4. Permitted Usage
Equipment may be used only within the IISER Kolkata campus and its immediate surroundings
for academic, cultural, or club-related activities unless prior approval is obtained.
5. Damage or Loss
Borrowers assume full responsibility for the equipment during the lending period. Any repair or
replacement costs arising from loss or damage shall be borne by the borrower as determined by the
club office bearers.
6. Return Policy
Equipment must be returned by the agreed date and time. The maximum borrowing period is 12
days unless an extension has been approved by the club office bearers.
7. Late Return
A penalty of Rs. 50 per day will be charged for overdue returns. Repeated violations may result
in suspension of borrowing privileges.
