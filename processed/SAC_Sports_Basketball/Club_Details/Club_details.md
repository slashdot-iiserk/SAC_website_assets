# Club Details

ISCOBAC (IISER Kolkata Basketball Club)


### Short Introduction


ISCOBAC (IISER Kolkata Basketball Club) is the official basketball club of IISER Kolkata, dedicated to promoting the sport of basketball and fostering a vibrant sporting culture within the institute. The club serves as a platform for students to develop their athletic abilities, teamwork, leadership skills, and sportsmanship while representing IISER Kolkata in various inter-college and inter-IISER competitions.


### Objectives


- To promote the game of basketball among students of IISER Kolkata.

- To provide regular training and skill-development opportunities for players of all experience levels.

- To encourage physical fitness, teamwork, discipline, and leadership through sports.

- To build a competitive basketball team capable of representing the institute at regional and national tournaments.

- To create an inclusive and engaging environment where students can learn, play, and enjoy the sport.


### Major Activities


- Regular practice sessions and coaching programs for club members.

- Selection and training of institute teams for inter-college and inter-IISER tournaments.

- Organization of intra-campus basketball competitions and friendly matches.

- Conducting introductory sessions and recruitment drives for new students.

- Participation in sports festivals, invitational tournaments, and institute-level sporting events.

- Promoting basketball culture through workshops, exhibitions, and community engagement activities.


### Major Achievements


- Consistent participation in Inter-IISER Sports Meets and various inter-college basketball tournaments.

- Representation of IISER Kolkata in competitive basketball events at various intercollege friendly matches.

- Development of a strong basketball community within the institute, encouraging student participation and athletic excellence.

- Successful organization of basketball events and competitions that have contributed to the growth of sports culture on campus.

- A well trained basketball team under a great coach is what we have as our power.


ISCOBAC continues to strive towards excellence in basketball while fostering a spirit of teamwork, dedication, and sportsmanship among the students of IISER Kolkata.
