# Self Defense and Yoga Club

Club Description

The club consists of two components, self-defense and yoga.

Self-defense wing

The self-defense wing works upon forging one to be lethal competent combatants to protect oneself if need be and professionally compete in combat sports like boxing, wrestling, taekwondo, bjj , judo. The main emphasis is on grappling heavy MMA.

We hold regular sparing sessions (twice a week) for grappling and submission of wrestling.

Sparrings for striking sports are once a week, and we follow a strict policy of technical sparring only for any striking sports (basically no hard hits).

Please don't worry, no one is hurt here, we have every possible equipment for protection, all the participants are mindful and are looked after by senior practitioners and it's quite fun to do.

Practice makes you practically untouchable in unarmed hand to hand combat.

Inter-batch grappling events are held, and new events are also being planned.

Yoga wing

The Yoga wing works for overall holistic development of the body to make members capable of handling academic and personal challenges. The events are aimed at developing the emotional quotient of the students, which gives a complement to the general academic curriculum that is being offered at IISER Kolkata.

Regular Yoga and Meditation practice, with active participation from the BS-MS students as well as PhD students and faculty members.

Participation in International Yoga day.
