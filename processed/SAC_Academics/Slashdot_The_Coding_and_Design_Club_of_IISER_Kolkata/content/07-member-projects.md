## The PhyXit Bot

Built by Rohan Kumar (21MS), Sattwamo Ghosh (21MS), Apuroop Vaidyam (21MS), Priyanshu Mahato (21MS), and Tushar Gayan (19MS).

PhyXit is a Discord bot for physics enthusiasts. It can simulate physical systems, fetch ArXiv papers, summarise Wikipedia articles, and pull up a professor's Google Scholar profile with ease.
