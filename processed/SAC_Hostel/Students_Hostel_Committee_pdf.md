# Students Hostel Committee

## Page 1

Students Hostel Committee {SHC} 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1.​ Each floor of every wing in each hostel shall have one Wing 
Representative. 
2.​ Each sub-committee shall consist of 5 members: 1 Convenor, 1 Associate 
Convenor, and 3 Coordinators. The hierarchy within the sub-committee 
shall be as follows: 
Convenor > Associate Convenor > Coordinators 
 
3.​ Email id of Hostel committee (for both wing representative and sub committee): 
hostelcommittee@iiserkol.ac.in
