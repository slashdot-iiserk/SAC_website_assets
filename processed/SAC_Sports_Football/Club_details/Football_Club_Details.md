# Football Club Details

Football Club of IISER Kolkata (FCIK)

Club Details


## Introduction

The Football Club of IISER Kolkata is dedicated to the continuous improvement of the footballing culture at IISER Kolkata. The club aims to serve every student with a passion for the game of football, be it through playing or watching the sport. We are responsible for various aspects of football at IISER Kolkata, like ground maintenance, match screenings and proper maintenance of equipment so that everyone who wants to fall in love with the beautiful game can do so, be it students, professors or staff!


## Objectives

To help active football players pursue the sport well, with a well-maintained football ground and properly sourced equipment (football, cones, first aid kit, etc.)

To promote football and its health benefits among everyone, be it a seasoned player or a beginner, especially among girls

To help train both boys’ and girls’ football teams to represent the institute at various tournaments, most importantly the Inter-IISER Sports Meet (IISM)

To promote sportsmanship and make sure that players fight for the win but always in good faith

To ensure that football fanatics in the institute get opportunities to witness big matches on the big screen, together, by screening important matches

To provide people a platform to discuss and enjoy the beautiful game


## Major Activities

Regular coaching sessions for both boys and girls as well as regular practice matches within the institute as well as against external teams, leading up to team selection for the IISM teams for boys and girls

Conduct of interbatch and auction tournaments for both boys and girls

Conduct of freshers’ matches for incoming batch, and farewell matches for outgoing batch (both boys and girls)

Screening of important football matches for everyone to watch

Ground maintenance and maintaining stock of important equipment like footballs, cones, corner flags, first aid, etc.
