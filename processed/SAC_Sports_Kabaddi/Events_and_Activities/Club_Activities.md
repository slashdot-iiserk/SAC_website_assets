# Club Activities

### Club Activities

The Kabaddi Club is committed to promoting teamwork, sportsmanship, and competitive excellence through a variety of activities conducted throughout the academic year. From welcoming new students to preparing athletes for inter-university competitions, the club provides opportunities for every member to develop their skills and represent the institute with pride.

Our major activities include:

Freshers' Match – A friendly yet competitive match between the incoming batch and their immediate senior batch, helping new students integrate into the club while fostering team spirit and camaraderie.

IISM Practice Sessions – Regular, structured training sessions focused on improving fitness, strategy, and match performance in preparation for the IISM.

Inter-Batch Tournament – A competitive tournament featuring teams from different academic batches, encouraging healthy competition, teamwork, and interaction among students while providing a platform for players to demonstrate their skills and gain valuable match experience.

Kabaddi Auction – An exciting annual event where players are selected into balanced teams through an auction-based format, creating a fun and competitive atmosphere before the club tournament.

Participation in Inter-University Tournaments – The club actively represents the institute in various tournaments hosted by other universities, providing members with valuable competitive exposure and opportunities to showcase their talent at a higher level.
