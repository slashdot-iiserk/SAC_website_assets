# Ikqc   Quiz Club Of Iiser Kolkata

IKQC - Quiz Club of IISER Kolkata

## Introduction

IKQC, the quiz club of IISER Kolkata, is a dynamic community of curious minds from different parts of India engaged in the pursuit of knowledge. It serves as a platform for students to explore a wide range of subjects, stay updated with current affairs, and engage in healthy competition through quizzes and knowledge-based activities. More than just a club, the Quiz Club is a space where learning becomes exciting, teamwork is celebrated, and knowledge is transformed into a lifelong asset.

## Events

Freshers’ Quiz - Held at the beginning of the academic year as an ice-breaking program for incoming juniors. Through an engaging mix of questions and friendly competition, the event helps students interact with one another and take their first steps into the institute’s vibrant quiz culture.

Dublin Wager - Organized as part of Lexis, the annual Literary Fest, Dublin Wager is one of the flagship events of the Quiz Club. This prestigious inter-college quiz competition brings together participants from various institutions and is conducted by a renowned quizmaster, offering an exciting platform for knowledge, wit, and competitive quizzing.

Inquizzitive - Held as part of Inquivesta, India’s largest college science fest, Inquizzitive is a general quiz competition that challenges participants across a broad spectrum of topics. The event fosters curiosity, quick thinking, and a spirit of healthy competition, attracting enthusiastic quizzers from across the country.
