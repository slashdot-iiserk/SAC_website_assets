# Events And Activities

CCIK (CARROM CLUB OF IISER KOLKATA)

IISER KOLKATA EVENTS AND ACTIVITIES

Events & Tournaments

1. Fresher Match

A welcome match organized for newly admitted students to introduce them to the carrom culture at IISER Kolkata and encourage participation in club activities.

2. IISM Practice Sessions

Structured training sessions conducted throughout the year to improve carrom fundamentals, tactics, critical thinking, and tournament readiness.

3. Practice Matches

Friendly matches are organized to provide competitive exposure and help players gain real-game pressure and experience.

4. IISM

Participation in the Inter IISER Sports Meet, where the IISER Kolkata carrom team represents the institute in national-level inter-IISER competition.

5. Interbatch Matches

An intra-campus tournament involving different academic batches, promoting healthy competition, interaction, and team spirit among students.

Club Activities

● Organizes regular carrom tournaments throughout the academic year, catering to players of all skill levels.

● Hosts Inter-Batch Carrom Championships to encourage participation and foster a competitive spirit among students.

● Holds practice sessions, friendly matches to help members improve their strategic and analytical skills.

● Prepares and trains students for inter-institute competitions such as IISM, ensuring strong representation of IISER Kolkata at national-level events.
