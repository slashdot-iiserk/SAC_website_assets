Intro of the Club

Nature club of IISER Kolkata, dedicated to fostering creative expression through photography and visual story telling.

The Club aims to:

Encourage interest in nature, ecology, and sustainable practices among students.

Organize Cleanliness drives, tree plantation drives, bird watching sessions, nature walks, and awareness campaigns.

Document biodiversity on campus and support conservation efforts in collaboration with other clubs or organizations.

Educating students about climate change and other important climate variables

Rules & Regulations

1. Equipment Lending Rules

The equipment remains the sole property of Nature Club and must only be used within or around IISER Kolkata. Taking equipment home or outside campus without permission is strictly prohibited.

Borrowers must submit a Google Form (linked below) along with a physical equipment lending form signed by an OB and the SAC Cultural GS before taking equipment.

If an OB is issuing equipment, the signature under the OB section must be from a different OB to ensure accountability. Two signatures on the form cannot be the same.

Borrowers must inspect the equipment for damages before taking it. Any existing issues should be reported to an OB immediately

The borrower is responsible for any damage or loss of equipment and may be required to compensate based on the severity of the issue.

Equipment must be returned within the agreed deadline (maximum borrowing period: 12 days).

Late returns will be charged a fine of 50 ₹ per day.

All photos and media taken with borrowed equipment are the sole responsibility of the borrower. Nature Club is not responsible for any loss of media after equipment is returned


2. Camera and Binoculars Handling & Storage

Camera, binoculars and lenses must be handled with care. Borrowers must

Keep equipment dry and avoid exposure to direct sunlight.

Use lens cap when camera and binoculars are not in use.

Return cameras to the dry cabinet in the DoSA office immediately after use.

Avoid unnecessary tampering with settings and accessories.

Only students with prior experience in handling cameras or those who have attended a basic handling session conducted by Pixel OBs will be allowed to borrow cameras.


3. Event Organisation Guidelines

All Nature Club events shall be registered and approved by SAC

External speakers, judges, or photographers invited for workshops shall be approved by DoSA.

Event funding shall be managed as per the budget sanctioned by SAC

Photography contests and exhibitions shall have clear submission guidelines, judging criteria, and prize distribution plan
