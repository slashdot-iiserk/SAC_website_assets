2. WEIGHT ROOM & EQUIPMENT MANAGEMENT
Weight Re-Racking
Always unload barbells and return dumbbells, kettlebells, and weight plates to their designated racks immediately 
after set completion. Leaving weights on the ﬂoor creates a severe safety hazard for others.
No Slamming Gear
Dropping heavy dumbbells or slamming weight stacks on cable machines/selectorized equipment causes 
structural damage and is strictly prohibited.
Equipment Sharing & Spotting
Allow fellow members to "work in" between sets during busy hours. Do not occupy benches or machines while 
resting or using mobile phones when others are waiting. Members are strongly encouraged to lift with a spotter 
when handling heavy weight.
Equipment & Floor Cleaning
You are encouraged to wipe down equipment pads, benches, and handles after your workout. Please ensure proper 
disposal of waste and keep the gym ﬂoor clean and tidy properly at all times.

3. MOTORIZED TREADMILLS & CARDIO GUIDELINES
Treadmill Operational Rules
1. Mandatory Footwear: Use dedicated clean indoor sports/running shoes while using the treadmill. Bare
feet, socks-only, slippers, crocs, or outdoor muddy shoes are strictly prohibited.
2. 15–20 Minute Time Limit: During peak hours, limit continuous treadmill sessions to a maximum of 20
minutes to allow equal access to all members.
3. Red Emergency Safety Key: The magnetic safety key must be clipped to your clothing prior to starting the
belt for auto-stop protection against slips.
4. Safe Shutdown: Gradually reduce belt speed to 0.0 km/h before stepping oﬀ. Jumping onto or oﬀ a fast-
moving belt is unsafe and strictly prohibited.
5. Console & Hygiene Protection: Avoid splashing water or dripping excessive sweat onto the digital display
touch controls. Wipe down the console, touch display, and side handrails after use.
4. DRESS CODE & HYGIENE POLICY
Footwear guidelines
Only barefoot or clean indoor sports/running shoes are permitted inside the gym.
Towel & Sweat Hygiene Guidelines
Members are encouraged/recommended to carry a dedicated workout towel and lay it over bench press pads, machine 
seats, and workout mats before training. Handkerchiefs or small hand towels are primarily for face wiping. Please keep 
the gym clean, maintain proper disposal of items, and avoid throwing trash around.
5. ENFORCEMENT & DISCIPLINARY POLICY
Violations & Membership Suspension
Non-compliance with safety regulations, intentional damage to gym equipment (including treadmills), throwing stuﬀ 
around, or misconduct towards gym guards and fellow members will result in immediate revocation of gym access 
privileges and reporting to the SAC Sports Committee.
