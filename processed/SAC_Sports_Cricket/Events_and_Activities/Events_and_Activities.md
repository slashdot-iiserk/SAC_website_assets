# Events And Activities

## Page 1

Major Events Organised by the Club
The Cricket Club of IISER Kolkata (IKCC) organises several 
flagship events throughout the academic year, providing students with 
opportunities to engage in competitive cricket, develop teamwork, and 
foster a vibrant sporting culture on campus.
Fresher's Cricket Match is one of the first sporting events organised 
each academic session. Conducted for both incoming male and female 
students, the event serves as a platform to welcome freshers and 
introduce them to the cricketing community at IISER Kolkata. It 
encourages interaction between freshers and immediate senior students, 
helping new members integrate into campus life while providing an 
opportunity to showcase their cricketing talent.
The Inter-Batch Cricket Tournament is among the most anticipated 
events of the academic calendar. The tournament features enthusiastic 
participation from students across batches, with separate competitions 
organised for men's and women's teams. Representing their respective 
batches, participants compete in a spirit of healthy rivalry and 
sportsmanship, fostering batch unity while displaying their cricketing 
skills.
The IISER-K Premier League (IPL) is the club's signature auction-
based cricket tournament. Inspired by the Indian Premier League 
format, the event begins with an exciting player auction in which team 
owners bid for players to build balanced squads. The tournament 
combines strategic team management with competitive cricket, creating 
an engaging experience for participants and spectators alike. The 
IISER-K Premier League has become one of the most popular sporting 
events on campus.
Inter IISER–NISER–CEBS Sports Meet (IISM), a prestigious annual 
sporting event that brings together students from IISERs, NISER, and 
CEBS. Representing IISER Kolkata at IISM provides student-athletes 
with the opportunity to compete at a high level, interact with peers from 
other institutions, and contribute to the institute's sporting 
achievements. The event plays a significant role in promoting inter-
institutional camaraderie and excellence in sports.

The Cricket Club of IISER-K
Farewell Cricket Match is a special event organized to celebrate 
and honor the graduating members of the IISER Kolkata cricketing 
community. The match serves as a memorable send-off for senior 
students, bringing together current and outgoing players for a day of 
friendly competition and camaraderie. Beyond the game itself, the 
event provides an opportunity to acknowledge the contributions of 
graduating students to the club and create lasting memories before 
they embark on the next stage of their academic and professional 
journeys.
