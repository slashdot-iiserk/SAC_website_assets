Gaming Club Rooms 
The Gaming Club currently uses three spaces for its activities: 
• 
LHC G02 – Used for our regular weekend casual gaming nights. The room is open to all 
students during announced club events. 
 
• 
LHC G08 – Used for gaming nights if the crowd is bigger than usual. 
 
 
• 
SAC 100 – Located in the SAC building. This is the club's dedicated gaming room and is 
currently accessible only to the current Office Bearers (OBs).
