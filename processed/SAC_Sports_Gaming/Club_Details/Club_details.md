GCIK(Gaming Club of IISER Kolkata) 
Introduction  
GCIK (Gaming Club of IISER Kolkata) is the official gaming club in IISER Kolkata 
that brings together gaming enthusiasts on campus. The club provides a 
platform for students to connect through video games, esports, and other 
interactive gaming activities, fostering both recreation and community building. 
The club is known for organising gaming nights, tournaments, and multiplayer 
gaming events that encourage participation from students across different 
batches. It also promotes teamwork, strategic thinking, and healthy 
competition while creating a fun and inclusive environment for students to 
unwind and connect with their peers. 
 
Activities: 
• Organising gaming nights and weekend gaming sessions. 
• Conducting esports tournaments and friendly competitions. 
• Promoting multiplayer and team-based gaming experiences. 
• Facilitating interactions among students with shared interests in gaming. 
• Participating in and encouraging inter-institute esports events. 
 
Objectives: 
• To create a vibrant gaming community within IISER Kolkata. 
• To provide students with a healthy recreational outlet alongside 
academics. 
• To develop teamwork, strategic thinking, and communication skills 
through gaming. 
• To promote esports and competitive gaming culture on campus. 
• To foster social interaction among students with diverse interests. 
• Promote Awareness of the Gaming and Esports Industry

Achivements                                             
• Successfully organised regular gaming nights, LAN events, and inter-
hostel tournaments, fostering collaboration, teamwork, and student 
engagement. 
• Conducted competitive esports tournaments featuring popular titles 
such as Valorant, BGMI, CS2, Marvel Rivals, and other multiplayer games, 
attracting widespread participation from the student community. 
• Played a key role in organising Headshot, the flagship esports event of 
Inquivesta, providing a competitive platform for gamers from multiple 
institutions. 
• Represented IISER Kolkata in inter-institute esports competitions and 
promoted participation in the growing Inter-IISER esports ecosystem. 
• Created an inclusive environment for gamers of all skill levels through 
mentoring, community events, and collaborative gameplay sessions.
