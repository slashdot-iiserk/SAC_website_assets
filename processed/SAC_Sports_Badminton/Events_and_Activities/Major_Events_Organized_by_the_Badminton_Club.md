# Major Events Organized By The Badminton Club

Major Events Organized by the Badminton Club



### Fresher’s Tournament

The Fresher’s Tournament is an introductory badminton competition organized exclusively for new students from BS-MS, IP, and MS batches. The event provides a friendly yet competitive environment where freshers can experience structured gameplay, understand match rules, and integrate into the badminton community. It also helps identify promising new talent for advanced training and future competitions.


### Beginner’s Tournament

The Beginner’s Tournament is designed for players who are relatively new to badminton or have limited competitive experience. The event focuses on participation, learning match etiquette, and improving basic gameplay skills through structured matches. It encourages confidence building and promotes inclusivity in sports participation within the campus community.


### Pro’s Tournament

The Pro’s Tournament is a high-level intra-club competition featuring experienced and skilled badminton players. The event showcases advanced gameplay, strategy, speed, and endurance. It serves as a platform for selecting top-performing players for inter-college competitions and strengthening the overall competitive badminton culture in the institute.


### IISM Selection Match and Coaching

This event is conducted to identify and train players representing the institute in the IISM (Inter IISER Sports Meet). It includes selection trials followed by structured coaching sessions to improve technical skills, match strategies, and team coordination. The focus is on preparing a strong contingent for inter-institutional competition.


### Interbatch Tournament

The Interbatch Tournament is a competitive event conducted between different academic batches within the institute. It promotes batch spirit, teamwork, and healthy rivalry while providing a structured competitive platform for players across skill levels. The tournament helps in strengthening intra-campus sports engagement.


### Auction Tournament

The Auction Tournament is a unique format where teams are formed through a player auction system. Captains bid for players to create balanced teams, followed by a league-style competition. This format encourages strategy, team-building, and dynamic matchups, making the event highly engaging and competitive.


### Farewell Match

The Farewell Match is a special event organized to celebrate and honor graduating students of the badminton club. It is a friendly exhibition-style match that highlights their contribution to the club while creating a memorable and emotional sporting farewell. The event focuses on sportsmanship, appreciation, and community bonding.
