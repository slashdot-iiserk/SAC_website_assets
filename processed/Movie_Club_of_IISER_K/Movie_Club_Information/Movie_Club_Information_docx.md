# Movie Club Information

Intro of the Club

Movie Club is set to provide all enthusiastic people here at IISER K with an exquisite taste in movies.

We stream, we craft, we develop films, believing in the idea that if you love movies enough, you can make a good one. A club which brings people from different backgrounds, cultures, tastes and social skills together, under the banner of love for movies.

Email: movie.activity@iiserkol.ac.in
