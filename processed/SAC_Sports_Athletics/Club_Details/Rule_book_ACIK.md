# Rule Book Acik

Rule book



All IISER Kolkata students are eligible to participate in club activities.

Use sports equipment responsibly and return all equipment to its designated place after use.

Report any damaged or missing equipment immediately.

Keep the athletic ground clean and do not litter. Use the designated dustbins.

Do not damage the ground, track, field, or sports equipment.

Switch off lights and other electrical equipment after use, unless someone else is using the facility.

Maintain discipline and good sportsmanship at all times.

Harassment, bullying, cheating, or misuse of club property is strictly prohibited.
