Club Activities
The Kho-Kho Club of IISER Kolkata is dedicated to promoting the sport of kho-kho through a 
comprehensive annual plan designed to foster skill development, competitive spirit, and a strong 
sense of community among its members. Our activities cater to all levels of experience, from 
welcoming new students to celebrating our graduating athletes.
Events and activities for the year:
Fresher Match: A warm welcome for our new students, introducing them to the fundamentals of 
kho-kho, building essential chasing and dodging skills, and encouraging their active involvement 
in the club.
Practice Sessions: Throughout the year, we conduct structured training and conditioning 
sessions aimed at enhancing speed, sprinting endurance, reflex times, and overall athletic 
performance on the field.
Practice Matches : We engage in friendly matches with neighboring institutions to gain 
competitive exposure, refine defensive strategies, and prepare players for the dynamics of real 
game situations.
IISM (Inter IISER Sports Meet): Our kho-kho team proudly represents IISER Kolkata at this 
prestigious national-level tournament, competing against teams from other IISERs.
Interbatch Matches: This intra-campus tournament pits different academic batches against each 
other, cultivating healthy competition, batch pride, and robust team camaraderie.
All-Star Matches: Exhibition games that showcase the exceptional talents of our best chasers 
and runners from various batches, celebrating individual prowess and strengthening our club's 
community bonds.
Auction Tournament (Tentative): A unique, franchise-style tournament where team managers 
select players through a live auction, promising highly balanced teams and an intensely 
competitive experience.
Farewell Matches: Special events dedicated to honoring our graduating students, 
acknowledging their commitment, leadership, and invaluable contributions to the kho-kho club.
