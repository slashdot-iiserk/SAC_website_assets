About KCIK
The Kho-Kho Club of IISER Kolkata (KCIK) is a vibrant, student-run sports community dedicated to 
preserving and promoting the rich heritage of the traditional Indian sport, Kho-Kho. With a strong focus 
on regular training, strategic gameplay, and rigorous conditioning, KCIK actively nurtures talent and 
fosters a passion for the sport within the IISER Kolkata campus.
Major Achievements
IISM Champions
In a triumphant moment for the club, the KCIK 
men's squad clinched the Gold Medal at the Inter-
IISER Sports Meet (IISM). This victory marked a 
significant milestone, breaking an 11-year drought 
for the men's team and showcasing their dedication 
and skill. The win has reignited the spirit of Kho-
Kho at IISER Kolkata.
National Presence
KCIK takes pride in its expanding national footprint. 
The club actively trains and supports both men's 
and women's contingents to participate in 
various national-level Kho-Kho events. This 
commitment not only provides invaluable exposure 
to our players but also proudly represents IISER 
Kolkata on a broader sporting stage.
Campus Infrastructure
KCIK boasts a dedicated and well-maintained training facility: the IISER Kolkata Kho-Kho Ground. 
Located in the Haringhata Farm area, this ground is illuminated, providing an ideal environment for 
regular practice sessions, inter-batch games, and official tournaments hosted by the club. The facility is 
instrumental in the continuous development of our players.
