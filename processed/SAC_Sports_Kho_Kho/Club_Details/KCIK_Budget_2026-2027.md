1. Referee Charges 
●​ Charges for 1 referee for 1 day = ₹750 
●​ Charges for 2 referees for 1 day = ₹1,500 
●​ Number of match days = 15

●​ Budget for 1 referee = ₹750 × 15 = ₹11,250 
●​ Budget for 2 referees = ₹11,250 × 2 = ₹22,500
