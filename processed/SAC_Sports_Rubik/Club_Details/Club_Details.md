# Club Details

### Origins

Established in 2018 with Siddikanth Mishra as one of its foundational members of this club which was born out of a shared passion to promote Speedcubing (the art of speed-solving the Rubik’s Cube and other twisty puzzles). The mission from day one has been to foster a vibrant cubing culture not only among the students of IISER Kolkata, but also across colleges throughout West Bengal and India.

### The Pandemic Era & The Birth of AICS

When the COVID-19 pandemic forced everyone into isolation, the club refused to let the community drift apart. To keep the competitive spirit alive, the AICS (All IISER Cubing Society) was created. This initiative bridged the gap between cubers across all IISERs, NISER and IISc, hosting a series of dynamic online events, virtual competitions, and meetups that kept the community connected and thriving during challenging times.

### The Post-COVID Revival

As campuses reopened and transitioned back to normalcy in 2022, Yash Jakhmola stepped up to revive the club with resurgence of WCA tournaments. He rebuilt the club's campus presence, and set the stage for the next generation of cubers.

### Our Flagship Events & Competitions

The club maintains a blend of activities:

Freshers & Interbatch Tournaments: Perfect for welcoming new talent to the campus and fostering friendly, high-energy rivalry across different batches.

Inquivesta Cube Open: As part of IISERK’s flagship science festival Inquivesta, the club hosts an official WCA (World Cube Association) event.

IISERK Spring & Autumn Cube Opens: This is the signature seasonal WCA tournaments conducted across each semester.
