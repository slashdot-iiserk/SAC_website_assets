VOLIK
Volleyball club of IISER Kolkata
Events & Activities
We organize a variety of events throughout the year to foster sportsmanship, build community, and elevate
the volleyball culture at IISER Kolkata. Here are some of our flagship activities:
• National Sports Day: A day dedicated to celebrating fitness and unity. We host friendly matches
between students, encouraging everyone to step onto the court, celebrate the spirit of sports, and enjoy
the game in a relaxed, festive atmosphere.
• Freshers’ Match: This is our way of welcoming the newest members of the campus. We introduce them
to our vibrant court culture, helping them find their footing and passion for the sport early on.
• Interbatch Tournament (Boys & Girls): An intense and highly anticipated rivalry! This tournament
brings different batches head-to-head in a competitive tussel, showcasing the grit, skill, and batch pride
of our students on the volleyball court.
• Super Smash League (SSL): The ultimate men’s competition on campus. This is an exciting auction-
style tournament where teams are carefully built, bringing in professional-level strategy and competitive
thrills to the campus volleyball scene.
• Women’s Super Smash League (WSSL): Modeled after our signature league, the WSSL highlights
the incredible talent and competitive prowess of our female players. It serves as a premier platform for
showcasing high-level women’s volleyball at IISER Kolkata.
• All-Stars Match (Boys & Girls): This match pits the seasoned IISM veterans against the top-
performing ’Icon’ players of the campus. It is a display of pure athleticism and technical brilliance that
keeps the crowd on the edge of their seats.
• IISM (Inter IISER Sports Meet) & Training: As the representative body for volleyball, we take
pride in preparing our athletes for the Inter IISER Sports Meet. We conduct rigorous coaching sessions
throughout the year to refine skills, build team cohesion, and ensure our campus is ready to compete at
the highest level.
