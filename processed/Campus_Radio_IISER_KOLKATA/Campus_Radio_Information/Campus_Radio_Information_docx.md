# Campus Radio Information

Intro of the Club

By the students, for the students.

IISER Kolkata Campus Radio (IKCR) is the definitive voice of the student community. From the vibrant celebrations of campus culture to the pressing issues thar affect our daily lives, we are here to bridge the gap. Whether its highlighting student grievances, addressing administrative policies, or capturing the unique, everyday occurrences around the campus. If it happens at IISER K you’ll hear it here.

Unfiltered, unbiased, and unapologetically student-first. Tune in to stay connected.
